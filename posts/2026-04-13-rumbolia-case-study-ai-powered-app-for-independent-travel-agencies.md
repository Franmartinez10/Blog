# Rumbolia Case Study: AI-Powered App for Independent Travel Agencies

# Rumbolia Case Study

**Project**: Rumbolia — SaaS platform for independent travel agencies  
**Development date**: January 2026 (14-day sprint)  
**Built by**: Estudio Salvatierra  
**Tech stack**: Firebase, NLP, PWA, Service Workers

## Problem Statement

In 2025, 70% of independent travel agencies in Spain had no CRM or digital tool to deliver itineraries. Clients received PDFs via WhatsApp and compared them to real apps from OTAs like Booking or eDreams. Result: loss of trust, lower retention, client churn.

## Solution

A platform that converts any itinerary into a mobile-first app in under 15 minutes:

- AI-powered import from Word, Excel, email, or ChatGPT
- PWA installation without App Store
- Full offline mode (photos, maps, PDFs)
- 100% white label (agency's brand, not Rumbolia's)
- Integrated pack catalog with direct WhatsApp CTA

## Results

### Viajes Ikigai (Tomelloso)
- Delivery time: 2h → 10min
- Client satisfaction: +40%
- Retention: 65%

### Pilot Program (12 agencies, 180 itineraries)
- Delivery time: 90min → 10min
- Post-sale inquiries: 8 → 2 per trip
- Recommendation rate: 52% → 78%

## Technical Architecture

- **Backend**: Firebase (Google Cloud)
- **NLP**: Custom itinerary parser
- **Offline**: Service Workers + IndexedDB
- **Frontend**: React + PWA capabilities
- **Deployment**: Vercel + Firebase Hosting

## What's Next

- Cruise Mode (April 2026): AI interprets ship schedules
- LATAM expansion (Q3 2026)

## Conclusion

Rumbolia proves applied AI doesn't need complexity to be transformative. In 14 days, we built a tool that lets a 3-person agency compete in customer experience with eDreams or Booking.
