# My AI content pipeline to rank in Google and AI engines across platforms

Most people still think SEO is about writing articles.

It’s not.

SEO today is about building a distributed content system that generates authority across multiple platforms — not just your website.

This is the exact system I’ve been building to position my agency, [Salvatierra Digital](https://salvatierra.digital/), as a reference in AI Search Optimization.

And it’s working.

---

## The real shift: from content to systems

Publishing one article on your website is no longer enough.

Search engines — and more importantly AI engines like ChatGPT, Gemini, or other answer engines — don’t just rank pages.

They extract signals from multiple sources.

That means:

- Mentions  
- Links  
- Context  
- Repetition across platforms  
- Consistency of entities and profiles  

So instead of thinking:

> “I need to write a blog post.”

You need to think:

> “I need to deploy a content system.”

---

## Step 1: I search everything in English

One of the first strategic decisions I made was simple:

> I search everything in English.

Why?

Because the best platforms, the strongest publishing ecosystems, and most of the cleanest link opportunities are still English-first.

## Step 4: I adapt content for each platform

I don’t copy and paste the same article everywhere.

That is lazy, and it usually performs worse.

What I do is adapt the same core idea to different formats and expectations.

For example:

- **LinkedIn** works better for a more narrative or opinion-driven angle  
- **Dev.to** performs better with practical, technical, and structured content  
- **Hashnode** is useful for technical notes and engineering-focused articles  
- **Medium** supports more editorial and reflective pieces  
- **GitHub** helps reinforce technical credibility through repos, READMEs, and documentation  
- **Reddit** works through comments, explanations, and contextual participation rather than direct promotion  

So the strategy is not duplication.

It is transmedia adaptation.

One topic. Multiple surfaces. Different executions.

This creates a wider and more natural digital footprint around the same themes.

## Step 7: I am building a dashboard to automate the process

Doing all of this manually does not scale.

That is why I’m building a dashboard to centralize the entire workflow.

The idea is simple:

- Generate article drafts with AI  
- Structure everything in JSON and Markdown  
- Adapt outputs depending on the platform  
- Track published and pending pieces  
- Detect missing content opportunities  
- Keep the whole content system connected  

Instead of thinking article by article, I think in terms of infrastructure.

That is where the real leverage appears.
